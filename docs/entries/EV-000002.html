Second autocorrelation constant C2, lower bound · Autocorrelation inequalities · status current

Definition

C2 = sup over nonnegative f of ||f*f||_2^2 / (||f*f||_1 ||f*f||_inf). Entries record the best lower bound (largest ratio attained by an explicit f).

C_2=\sup_{f\ge0}\frac{\|f*f\|_2^2}{\|f*f\|_1\,\|f*f\|_\infty}

Domain: nonnegative functions on the real line. Normalisation: dimensionless ratio. Direction: max.
