EV-000829

Packing 6 unit circles in the smallest circle · Packing · status current

Definition

r(6) = the smallest radius of a circle that contains 6 non-overlapping circles of radius 1.

r(6)=\min\{r:\ 6\text{ disjoint open unit disks fit in a disk of radius }r\}

Domain: arrangements of unit disks in a disk. Normalisation: radius of the container circle; circles have radius 1; a trailing '+' marks a decimal printed truncated (true value slightly larger). Direction: min.
