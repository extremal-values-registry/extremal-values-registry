Sphere packing density in dimension 17 · Sphere packing and kissing · status current

Definition

Delta_17 = the supremum of the upper density of packings of congruent balls in R^17.

\Delta_{17}=\sup_{P}\ \limsup_{r\to\infty}\frac{\mathrm{vol}(P\cap B_r)}{\mathrm{vol}(B_r)}

Domain: packings of congruent balls in R^17. Normalisation: fraction of space covered; lower bounds printed truncated to 16 decimals, upper bounds rounded up. Direction: unstated.
