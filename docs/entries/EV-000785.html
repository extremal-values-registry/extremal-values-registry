EV-000785

Classical two-colour Ramsey number R(8,10) · Ramsey theory · status current

Definition

R(8,10) is the least n such that every 2-colouring of the edges of the complete graph K_n contains a K_8 in the first colour or a K_10 in the second colour.

R(8,10)=\min\{n:\ \forall\,c:E(K_n)\to\{1,2\},\ K_{8}\subseteq c^{-1}(1)\ \text{or}\ K_{10}\subseteq c^{-1}(2)\}

Domain: 2-colourings of the edges of complete graphs. Normalisation: number of vertices; a lower bound L means R > L-1 is shown by a colouring of K_{L-1}. Direction: unstated.

Current value

343 <= R(8,10) <= 4402 (bound)
